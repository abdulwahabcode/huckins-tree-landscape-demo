What we do

Tree Services

Every job starts with a walk-through of your property and an honest, written estimate — no pressure, no surprises.

01

Tree Removal

Safe, controlled removal of dead, diseased, or storm-damaged trees — including tight spots near homes, wires, and driveways. We clean up like we were never there.

02

Trimming & Pruning

Proper cuts that keep trees healthy and shapely — clearing branches from roofs, gutters, and power lines while opening up light and views.

03

Storm Damage Cleanup

Fast response after New Hampshire storms: downed limbs removed, tangled canopies untangled, and hazardous hangers cut down before they fall.

04

Stump Grinding

Old stumps ground down below grade so you can mow, plant, or seed right over them — chips raked out and hauled away.

05

Land & Lot Clearing

From overgrown edges to full building lots — brush, saplings, and unwanted trees cleared to give you back usable land.

06

Seasonal Tree Care

Winter pruning, ice-damage checks, and cabling for at-risk limbs — year-round care that keeps your trees standing strong through every season.
