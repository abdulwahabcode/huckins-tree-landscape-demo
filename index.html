What we do

Lawn Services

One visit or all season long — every service includes a full cleanup so your yard is ready to enjoy the moment we leave.

🌱

Weekly & Bi-Weekly Mowing

Consistent cuts at the right height for Wisconsin grass — bagged or mulched, with clippings never left behind.

✂

Edging & Trimming

Crisp lines along sidewalks, driveways, and beds. The detail work that makes a good mow look great.

🌿

Fertilization & Weed Control

Seasonally timed treatments that feed your lawn and crowd out dandelions and crabgrass — thick, green, and even.

🍂

Spring & Fall Cleanups

Leaf removal, stick pickup, and bed cleanup that resets your yard after winter and before it — ready for the next season.

Core aeration and overseeding in early fall to thicken thin spots and give tired lawns a fresh start.

❄

Shrub & Bed Touch-Ups

Light shrub trimming and mulch bed tidying to keep the whole front of your home looking cared for.
